About

Hey, I'm Joel

25, London. Trainee accountant by day, filming my life the rest of the time.

I'm the type of person who genuinely enjoys trying new things, keeping a structured routine (which inevitably never goes to plan), and finding excuses to buy another gadget I definitely don't need. Most importantly, I am a football and Arsenal fanatic.

Friends would describe me as organised, reliable, a perfectionist with a splash of chaos and ADHD.

I make content that feels like something you'd actually stop on — no forced influencer energy, no over-explaining. Just my real, authentic life.

When I'm not filming you'll find me cooking something, at the gym, or overthinking a video idea that didn't need a spreadsheet but got one anyway.
